Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Grace Alarm ("we," "our," or "the app") is a free Android application that wakes users with soft music and a Bible verse read aloud via text-to-speech. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Grace Alarm collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

2. Data We Collect

Grace Alarm is designed to work 100% offline. All core features — alarm scheduling, verse selection, text-to-speech playback, and settings — function entirely on your device without any internet connection.

2.1 Local Data (Stored Only on Your Device)

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Alarm times and on/off states
  • Verse mode selection (Random or Theme-based)
  • Selected themes (e.g., Faith, Hope, Love)
  • Bible translation preference (KJV, NKJV, NIV, NLT)
  • Snooze duration preference
  • Dark mode setting
  • Recent verse history (to prevent repeats)

2.2 Analytics Data (Anonymised, Optional)

Grace Alarm uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google) to collect anonymised usage data. This helps us understand how the app is used and improve the experience. The following data may be collected:

  • How often alarms are created and deleted (frequency counts only)
  • Which verse themes are most popular (aggregated, not per-user)
  • Verse mode preference (Random vs. Theme-based)
  • App crash reports and error logs
  • Session length and screen views
  • Device type, Android version, and app version

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

Grace Alarm does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location (GPS, network, or IP-based)
  • Your contacts, photos, or files
  • The specific Bible verses shown to you
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII)

4. How We Use Data

Anonymised analytics data is used exclusively to:

  • Understand which features are most used
  • Identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Make informed decisions about future improvements

We do not sell, share, or rent your data to any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Grace Alarm uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics — Anonymised usage analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Crash reporting to improve app stability. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Android Text-to-Speech — Built into your device. No data is sent to external servers.

6. Permissions

Grace Alarm requests only the permissions strictly required for its features:

  • Exact Alarms — To fire alarms at the correct time
  • Notifications — To alert you when alarms fire or are missed
  • Do Not Disturb Override — To sound alarms even in DND mode
  • Boot Completed — To re-register alarms after device restart
  • Internet — Only used for Firebase Analytics (queued when offline)
  • Vibrate — For alarm vibration feedback

Grace Alarm does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, storage, or microphone.

7. Data Storage & Security

All user-created data (alarms, settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database and DataStore. This data:

  • Survives app restarts and device reboots
  • Persists through app updates
  • Is never backed up to any cloud service by the app
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

8. Children's Privacy

Grace Alarm is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at the email below.

9. Your Choices

  • Disable Analytics: You can opt out of Firebase Analytics by disabling "Usage & diagnostics" in your Android device settings.
  • Delete Data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Permissions: You can revoke any permission at any time through your device's app settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.
